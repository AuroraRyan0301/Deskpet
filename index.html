Character

The three built-in characters are drawn procedurally and ship no third-party art. Drop a sprite-sheet pack in characters/, list it in characters/index.json, and it appears in this menu. Any action you add to a pack joins the vocabulary handed to the model on its own — no code change needed.

Experiment variables

Lower means fussier. At 0.28 your face has to drop 5.6% of the frame height and stay there for 1.6 s before it counts as slouching. The exit threshold is half that, so the reading cannot flap on the boundary.
Lower is not automatically better. The 04/30 lecture puts the visuo-tactile simultaneity window at ≈60 ms, with perceived conflict peaking at 30–120 ms of delay. This slider is the axis an evaluation would sweep.

Settings live in localStorage. With the slow loop on, camera frames leave this machine — they go to whatever endpoint you enter. Point it at a third-party relay and the fast loop's "nothing leaves the device" no longer holds. Point it at a local vLLM / llama.cpp / LM Studio / Ollama endpoint and everything stays local. A free GPT_API_free tier is 100 calls a day, which one call every 4 s burns through in seven minutes — which is why the quota guard is mandatory, not optional.
